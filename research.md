# Research

My research experience combines applied microeconomics, causal inference, ageing research, and long-term-care policy analysis using harmonized cross-country data.

# Research Experience and Fellowships

## Research Grant Holder — Jan. 2025–Present
**Department of Economics, Ca’ Foscari University of Venice**, Venice, Italy  
**Supervisors:** Agar Brugiavini and Giacomo Pasini

- Contribute to a research project on the long-run effects of unilateral divorce laws on later-life cognitive outcomes using SHARE data.
- Conduct empirical analyses of policy reforms using modern causal-inference methods and harmonized cross-country ageing data.
- Prepare policy reports on long-term-care systems for the Gateway to Global Aging Data.

## VERA Research Grant Holder — Jun. 2023–Oct. 2023
**Venice Centre in Economic and Risk Analytics for Public Policies, Ca’ Foscari University of Venice**, Venice, Italy  
**Supervisors:** Danilo Cavapozzi and Giacomo Pasini

- Prepared and managed harmonized panel datasets for cross-country empirical analysis.
- Conducted descriptive and regression analyses of ageing, family structure, and policy outcomes.
