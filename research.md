# Research Grants

<p class="page-intro">I have been awarded research grants at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice supporting applied work in ageing, causal inference, family economics, and long-term-care policy.</p>

## Awarded Grants

<div class="entry">
  <h3>Research Grant · Department of Economics</h3>
  <p class="entry-meta">Ca’ Foscari University of Venice · Venice, Italy · January 2025–Present</p>
  <p>Supervisors: Agar Brugiavini and Giacomo Pasini.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Contribute to research on the long-run effects of unilateral divorce laws on later-life cognitive outcomes using SHARE data.</li>
    <li>Conduct empirical analyses of policy reforms using modern causal-inference methods and harmonized cross-country ageing data.</li>
    <li>Prepare policy reports on long-term-care systems for the Gateway to Global Aging Data.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="entry">
  <h3>VERA Research Grant · Venice Centre in Economic and Risk Analytics for Public Policies</h3>
  <p class="entry-meta">Ca’ Foscari University of Venice · Venice, Italy · June–October 2023</p>
  <p>Supervisors: Danilo Cavapozzi and Giacomo Pasini.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Prepared and managed harmonized panel datasets for cross-country empirical analysis.</li>
    <li>Conducted descriptive and regression analyses of ageing, family structure, and policy outcomes.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
