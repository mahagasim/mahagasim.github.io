# Maha Gasim

<p class="page-intro">I am a Research Fellow (borsa di ricerca) in the Department of Economics at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice. I study how institutions and policy shape health and wellbeing across the life course, with a focus on ageing, family and social policy, and long-term care. My academic path from mathematics and computer science to data analytics and empirical economics informs how I approach measurement, identification, and reproducible research.</p>
