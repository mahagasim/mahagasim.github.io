# Projects

<p class="page-intro">Selected applied projects demonstrating statistical modelling, data analysis, reproducible workflows, and responsible use of real-world data.</p>

<div class="project-entry">
  <div class="project-title">Factors Influencing Adult Obesity in the United States</div>
  <div class="project-meta">Health Data Science · BRFSS 2022 · R · Stata</div>

  <p>I analysed the 2022 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System to examine demographic, socioeconomic, behavioral, and health factors associated with adult obesity in the United States.</p>

  <p><span class="project-label">Methods:</span> descriptive and probability analysis; binary and multivariable logistic regression; multinomial and ordinal logistic regression; generalized linear mixed modelling with a state-level random intercept; AIC/BIC and likelihood-ratio model comparison.</p>

  <p><span class="project-label">Data:</span> 445,132 observations in the initial BRFSS working extract and 39,551 observations in the final analytical sample. The source is public-use CDC data; large raw files are not stored in the repository.</p>

  <p><span class="project-label">Selected findings:</span> observed obesity prevalence was highest among adults aged 40–54 in the filtered sample, women had a higher observed rate than men, and the mixed-effects model captured residual state-level heterogeneity.</p>

  <p><span class="project-label">Project context:</span> originally completed as a Health Data Science group project with Amal Ahmed and Arnela Halili; I later reorganized and audited the materials for reproducibility and portfolio presentation.</p>
